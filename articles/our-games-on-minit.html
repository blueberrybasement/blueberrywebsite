Our Games on Minit: Play the Blueberry Basement Collection

What do you feel like doing for the next few minutes? Match a robot by its antenna, steer a cart full of parcels or slide meetings until the calendar fits. Each game below links straight to a playable Minit Post.

Meeting Mayhem: make the calendar fit

Meeting Mayhem is a scheduling puzzle. Drag movable meetings into available time, account for locked blocks and remove the overlaps. You need space for the entire meeting, so a useful first step is to compare long blocks with the gaps that can actually hold them.

The Minit adaptation uses compact selections of authored calendars. Different Posts can change the selected puzzles, move allowance and time budget. Play Meeting Mayhem: Fresh Start, or read how it differs from Android.

Lucky Lane: a board that becomes less forgiving

Roll a die to move around an L-shaped route, collecting gold as safe squares turn into dangerous skulls. Landing matters: passing over a skull is safe, but landing on one without protection ends the session. A shield can absorb a dangerous landing.

The challenge changes with the pace of new danger and the number and behaviour of shields. The linked Post offers several shields and a slower danger rhythm. Dice outcomes still matter; protection does not make a run certain. Play Lucky Lane.

Ashen Ascent: the next landing is the puzzle

Aim a jump by dragging and releasing near the knight. The jump travels opposite the drag, with its strength determined by the gesture. Climb the ledges toward the crown while the view follows your progress upward.

The linked challenge uses wider ledges as an introduction to the climb. Coins offer extra opportunities along the way, but reaching the crown is the goal. Falling below the view ends the run. Play Ashen Ascent.

Rainbow Roll: control before speed

Steer a ball through a three-dimensional course of turns, bridges and ramps. There is no jump button: staying on the route depends on steering, momentum and the slopes. Letting go of the touch control brakes the ball, although gravity still acts on steep descents.

Coins tempt you away from the safest line. The linked Post contains fourteen sections; other challenges can change course length, width and ramp steepness. Play Rainbow Roll.

Riot Road: build and protect a squad

Drag sideways to steer a squad that fires automatically. Recruit soldiers, break obstacles to collect stronger guns and choose between supply opportunities. Pursuing enemies and bosses create reasons to move beyond simply collecting upgrades.

Watch the marked attack lanes and keep the squad out of danger while working toward the bosses. Different Posts can vary enemy pressure, supplies and layouts. Play Riot Road.

Cat Crossing: find your moment in the traffic

A little orange cat needs to cross a low-poly neighbourhood of pastel cars and grassy verges. Tap anywhere for one hop forward. You can wait on the grass to watch the traffic, then commit to the next crossing when a gap opens.

Each completed hop to a new row earns a point. Traffic becomes more demanding with distance, and one car hit ends the run. There is no timer or finish line: the challenge is how far you can go. Play Cat Crossing: First Paws, or read our Cat Crossing introduction.

Bot Clue: read the order and find three robots

Scan the belts for the part pictured in the order, then tap three robots that have it. Training Shift uses single-part clues, so you can focus on the requested feature. Some other Posts end with three full blueprints to match. Play Bot Clue: Training Shift. See our visual deduction puzzle guide for a worked example.

Crowd Clue: spot the right faces in a moving crowd

Look for the pictured feature as the portraits slide past, then tap three people who match. Some clues show two details, so spotting one feature only narrows the field. A wrong tap costs a life. Play Crowd Clue: First Edition.

Hollow Siege: break through the defenses

Drag to move the cannon and line up shots through rune gates. Intercept groups as they approach, avoid the marked enemy fire lane and destroy the defenses. Once the core is exposed, move into line with it. Play Hollow Siege: Still Rune.

Orbital Swarm: build a fleet under fire

Drag sideways to steer while your fleet fires. Shoot the numbered recruitment gates to grow the fleet and commit enough fire to break weapon crates. Dodging diving drones can mean giving up a shot at a supply crate. Play Orbital Swarm: First Contact.

Wool Tally: count the sheep you shear

Count the sheep you actually shear. A sheep in a cardboard box takes two taps: one to remove the box and one to shear. A disguised wolf jams the shears, so look for pointed ears, a long muzzle and a jagged tail before you tap. Play Wool Tally: First Shear.

Goblin Express: keep the parcels on the cart

The cart rolls on its own. Drag gently to choose a line through the valley, collect coins along the route and avoid obstacles. Sharp turns can tip the load, so give the parcels time to settle before changing direction again. Play Goblin Express.

Captcha Hell: finish the verification before time runs out

Tap the checkbox to start a timed human verification test. Work through the image selection, shape matching and distorted code entry before the shared timer runs out. Play Captcha Hell.

Which game should you start with?

Choose Meeting Mayhem if you want time to read a board and plan a sequence. Try Lucky Lane if you enjoy uncertainty and survival. Pick Ashen Ascent or Rainbow Roll for careful movement, Riot Road for continuous action, or Cat Crossing for traffic timing with room to pause between crossings. For a quick observation challenge, start with Bot Clue or Crowd Clue. Try Wool Tally if you want to track a running total while you play. Each link opens a particular published Post, so another Post of the same game may offer a different challenge.
