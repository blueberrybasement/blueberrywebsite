Meeting Mayhem Daily Tasks and Lucky Spinner Explained

Daily tasks track goals while you play Meeting Mayhem levels. The Lucky Spinner is a separate reward feature. Here is how to read task progress, collect rewards and understand the daily reset in the Android game.

Where daily tasks fit into the game

Meeting Mayhem’s main objective stays the same: move unlocked meetings until every calendar conflict is gone. Daily tasks add goals around that level play, such as completing levels or winning consecutive levels without failing.

The tasks and spinner become available as you progress and are accessed through the game’s menu. If you are still learning to drag meetings, start with the gameplay guide. The daily systems give you additional goals once you know how the board works.

Task progress and reward claims are different

The daily task screen tracks your progress toward its listed objectives. A level-completion task advances as you win levels. A consecutive-win task depends on a run of wins without a failure. Check the goal shown for each task rather than assuming every goal measures the same action.

When an objective is complete, its reward becomes claimable. You still need to use the claim action to receive it. A completed progress bar does not by itself mean the reward has already been added to your balance or inventory.

A useful way to finish a session is to reopen the task screen and check for claimable rewards. Daily rewards left unclaimed are lost when the next daily reset arrives. The reward displayed in the game is the one to check before claiming; this guide does not prescribe a fixed reward amount.

What happens if you fail a level?

A failure ends your current consecutive-win run. Daily task tracking also keeps your best run for the day. If you have already achieved the consecutive-win objective, a later failure does not erase that achievement before you claim its reward.

These are separate ideas: the streak you are currently building and the best streak you have already reached. When checking a task, look at whether its objective is complete rather than treating a recent failure as proof that all of today’s progress has disappeared.

The daily reset uses UTC

The mobile game uses the UTC calendar date for daily tasks. UTC midnight may occur before or after midnight in your local time zone. A task refresh can therefore happen at a different local hour from the one you expect.

Claim completed daily rewards before that rollover. The new day brings a fresh set of daily progress counters. A daily task is a goal attached to your play; it does not mean that Meeting Mayhem necessarily supplies one unique calendar puzzle for each date.

Daily tasks also contribute to weekly milestones

Completing daily objectives contributes to weekly task progress. Weekly milestones have their own reward states, so inspect that part of the task screen as well as the individual daily rows.

Daily claims and weekly progress are not interchangeable. Daily rewards need to be claimed before their daily rollover, while the weekly cycle resets on Monday in UTC. Use the milestone requirements and rewards displayed in your game to see what is available.

How the Lucky Spinner differs

The Lucky Spinner is a reward wheel, rather than another scheduling board. The mobile game includes free spins and additional spins supported by rewarded ads, with daily availability and a cooldown between spins.

Check the spinner screen for your available action before expecting another spin. Having a completed daily task does not mean a spin is ready: task progress, spinner availability and the cooldown are separate checks.

A short end-of-session check

  1. Open daily tasks and inspect which objectives are complete.
  2. Claim any daily rewards that are ready.
  3. Check weekly milestones for an available reward.
  4. If you want to use the spinner, check its current availability and cooldown.
