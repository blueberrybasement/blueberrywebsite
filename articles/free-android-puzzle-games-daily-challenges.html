By Blueberry Basement Published 8 Nov 2026

Free Android Puzzle Games with Daily Challenges and Missions

Pick Sudoku.com for a daily grid, Puzzle Page for a rotating mix, or Meeting Mayhem for missions you complete while playing regular levels. Here are five free-to-install games and what you can come back to do each day.

We checked the developers’ listings and support pages for each daily feature. Blueberry Basement makes Meeting Mayhem. The listings disclose ads and in-app purchases, and event availability can change. This comparison is based on documented features rather than a timed Android playtest.

Sudoku.com for a daily number grid

Start with the Daily Challenge if you want a specific grid to return to and a trophy to collect. A Sudoku board is one self-contained goal. If the day's grid looks too demanding for your break, choose an easier regular puzzle instead. The developer also lists seasonal events, so check the current schedule in the app.

Sudoku.com on Google Play

Blockudoku for a daily spatial challenge

Place shapes on a nine-by-nine board to clear lines or squares. Daily Challenges give you a trophy goal beyond beating your regular score. Choose the daily mode before you start if you want a specific objective. A high-score run can go on as long as you keep making space for new pieces.

Blockudoku on Google Play

Puzzle Page for a different mix each day

The daily page combines several puzzle types, including crosswords, Sudoku and nonograms. Pick a single puzzle or try the daily word challenge if you want a smaller commitment. The developer describes the free version as ad-supported and offers a subscription for more puzzles and subscriber benefits.

Puzzle Page on Google Play

Meeting Mayhem for missions inside regular levels

Our Android game asks you to drag meetings into free calendar slots. Daily goals can ask you to clear levels or win consecutively. Check the task screen before playing if you want to make progress toward a specific reward, then return to claim it. The daily task cycle also feeds weekly milestones.

Read the Meeting Mayhem daily tasks guide for unlocks, claiming and the UTC rollover.

Meeting Mayhem on Google Play

Two Dots for Daily Quests and rotating events

Two Dots gives you objectives on a board of colored dots. Its help center describes Daily Quests that reward playing unbeaten levels, so replaying a favorite board may be the wrong way to progress a quest. Check the Events tab for currently available challenges and their deadlines.

Two Dots on Google Play · Two Dots event and quest help

Weekly milestones and limited-time events

Choose a weekly milestone if you want small daily goals to build toward one reward. Meeting Mayhem’s weekly milestones work that way and reset on Monday in UTC. Choose a limited-time event if you want extra puzzles or a separate goal. Two Dots publishes event calendars, and Puzzle Page lists events with bonus rewards. Check the dates in the app before planning around a particular event.
