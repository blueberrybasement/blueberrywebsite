Our Games on Minit: Puzzles, Platformers and a Squad Shooter

Our Minit collection has five games built around different kinds of decisions. Start with Meeting Mayhem for planning, Lucky Lane for dice survival, Ashen Ascent or Rainbow Roll for precision, and Riot Road for action.

Meeting Mayhem: make the calendar fit

Meeting Mayhem is a scheduling puzzle. Drag movable meetings into available time, account for locked blocks and remove the overlaps. You need space for the entire meeting, so a useful first step is to compare long blocks with the gaps that can actually hold them.

The Minit adaptation uses compact selections of authored calendars. Different Posts can change the selected puzzles, move allowance and time budget. Play Meeting Mayhem: Fresh Start, or read how it differs from Android.

Lucky Lane: a board that becomes less forgiving

Roll a die to move around an L-shaped route, collecting gold as safe squares turn into dangerous skulls. Landing matters: passing over a skull is safe, but landing on one without protection ends the session. A shield can absorb a dangerous landing.

The challenge changes with the pace of new danger and the number and behaviour of shields. The linked Post offers several shields and a slower danger rhythm. Dice outcomes still matter; protection does not make a run certain. Play Lucky Lane.

Ashen Ascent: the next landing is the puzzle

Aim a jump by dragging and releasing near the knight. The jump travels opposite the drag, with its strength determined by the gesture. Climb the ledges toward the crown while the view follows your progress upward.

The linked challenge uses wider ledges as an introduction to the climb. Coins offer extra opportunities along the way, but reaching the crown is the goal. Falling below the view ends the run. Play Ashen Ascent.

Rainbow Roll: control before speed

Steer a ball through a three-dimensional course of turns, bridges and ramps. There is no jump button: staying on the route depends on steering, momentum and the slopes. Letting go of the touch control brakes the ball, although gravity still acts on steep descents.

Coins tempt you away from the safest line. The linked Post contains fourteen sections; other challenges can change course length, width and ramp steepness. Play Rainbow Roll.

Riot Road: build and protect a squad

Drag sideways to steer a squad that fires automatically. Recruit soldiers, break obstacles to collect stronger guns and choose between supply opportunities. Pursuing enemies and bosses create reasons to move beyond simply collecting upgrades.

Watch the marked attack lanes and keep the squad out of danger while working toward the bosses. Different Posts can vary enemy pressure, supplies and layouts. Play Riot Road.

Which game should you start with?

Choose Meeting Mayhem if you want time to read a board and plan a sequence. Try Lucky Lane if you enjoy uncertainty and survival. Pick the platformers for careful movement, or Riot Road for continuous action. Each link opens a particular published Post, so another Post with the same game name may offer a different challenge.
