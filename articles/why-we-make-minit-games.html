What Is Minit Games, and Why Are We Making Games for It?

Minit Games is a platform for short-form games, discovered in a feed. Blueberry Basement now makes games for Minit alongside Meeting Mayhem on Android: calendar puzzles, dice survival, climbing, ball rolling and squad shooting.

What is Minit Games?

Minit Games brings small, playable games into a feed. A Post is a particular playable challenge, rather than a store listing for an entire mobile game. Players can open a challenge, learn its central action and reach an ending within that session.

That structure changes what we build. A game needs to explain itself through the opening screen and the first interaction. It also needs a clear result: a solved calendar, a crown reached, a finish line crossed or a survival run that comes to an end.

Why Blueberry Basement is working on Minit

Our Android game, Meeting Mayhem, starts from one concrete idea: untangle a crowded calendar. Minit gives us room to explore other equally focused ideas without asking each one to support a whole mobile campaign. Lucky Lane asks how long you can survive a changing board. Ashen Ascent puts the next landing at the centre of every decision.

We are interested in that small unit of play. A short game can still ask for planning, timing or restraint. Rainbow Roll, for example, is about controlling a ball through turns and slopes; rushing ahead can make a simple-looking course much harder.

One game can offer different challenges

Our Minit games support settings that change the actual session. Lucky Lane can vary how frequently new danger appears and how shields behave. Ashen Ascent can change ledge widths and gaps. Meeting Mayhem can use a different selection and order of authored calendar puzzles.

These changes matter because the player faces different decisions. A shield that stays on one square creates a different route through risk from a shield that moves after collection. We describe such differences in Post captions so players can understand which challenge they are opening.

Short-form does not mean every run lasts exactly a minute

The important boundary in our games is a complete session. Length depends on the game, the chosen challenge and how someone plays. Some runs end on a failed jump; a calendar session can contain several puzzles. We do not describe a single duration as a promise for every title.

Where to try our games

The Blueberry Basement Minit collection links directly to published playable Posts. Open a game link on your device and follow Minit’s play flow. You do not need a separate download for each individual game; Minit handles access through its app or web player.

For the platform’s own explanation, visit Minit Games. For a tour of our titles, read our Minit game guide. Our Meeting Mayhem comparison explains how its compact adaptation differs from the Android app.
