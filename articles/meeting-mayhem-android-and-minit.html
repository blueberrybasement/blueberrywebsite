Meeting Mayhem on Android and Minit: What Changes?

Meeting Mayhem has an Android app on Google Play and a separate HTML5 adaptation on Minit Games. Both ask you to clear overlapping meetings, but the Minit edition packages selected puzzles into a self-contained session.

The calendar idea stays the same

In both versions, the calendar starts with conflicting meetings. Move the unlocked blocks into usable space while respecting the fixed events. The goal is to remove all overlaps, not to copy one prescribed arrangement.

Meeting lengths matter because a gap must fit the whole block. A short meeting may need to move first to free a destination for a longer one. These are the same planning problems discussed in our overlapping meeting puzzle guide.

Android: a mobile game with progression and rewards

The Android app surrounds the calendar puzzles with progression, daily tasks, the Lucky Spinner and power-ups. It is the version to choose if you want the broader mobile game and its reward systems. Install it from Meeting Mayhem on Google Play.

Our Android gameplay guide covers that edition’s rules and controls. The daily tasks and Lucky Spinner article also refers specifically to the Android app.

Minit: selected calendars in one session

The Minit adaptation has a different boundary: one Post contains a complete session using a selected sequence of calendars. Our configurable edition supports one to five boards per session. The Post determines their order, extra moves and time allowance.

Those calendars come from authored layouts in the original Unity project. We preserve their meeting positions, durations and locks rather than generating replacement boards. Selection changes which puzzles appear; it does not rearrange the original meetings into a new random level.

Why the surrounding features differ

A Minit Post needs to stand on its own. We therefore centre this adaptation on solving its chosen calendars and reaching a session result. The Android app’s daily tasks, spinner and ongoing progression are not features of this Minit edition.

Timing also needs context. The hours printed on a calendar describe scheduling space. In the Minit adaptation, a separate session or board countdown controls how long you have to solve the selected challenge. The calendar’s labels are not that countdown.

Which version should you play?

For a compact challenge, play Meeting Mayhem: Fresh Start on Minit. For the Android game with progression and daily reward features, use the official Google Play listing. These are separate ways to play; installing the Android app is not required to follow a Minit play link.
